About Me

My name is Kostas Powell. I’m currently pursuing a bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering as well as a certificate in Data Analytics at Queen’s University. I am proficient in CAD, GD&T, several programming languages, and ANSYS FEA/CFD.

I’m interested in mechanical design, aerospace systems, simulation, and data driven engineering. Through my projects, I’ve worked on things like hybrid rocket injector design, aircraft simulation, mechanical analysis, and hands on prototyping.

This portfolio is a collection of the projects, technical experience, and extracurricular work that has shaped me as an engineering student. I’m always looking to build things, learn new tools, and take on problems that combine creativity with real engineering.
